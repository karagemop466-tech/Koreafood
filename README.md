# Korea 2026 food map 🇰🇷

A practical, two-person food plan for **Seoul + Busan**, with **Cheonan (천안)** and **Daejeon (대전)** as the flex-city shortlist. Trip: **31 October–22 November 2026**.

> **Status:** research-first working guide, not a reservation list. Hours and prices change frequently in Korea. Every restaurant has a map/business link and a source link; entries marked `RECHECK` should be checked again 2–4 weeks before travel and on the day. Prices are in KRW and are generally per serving unless stated otherwise.

## How to use this guide

* **Best value:** target lunch, market food, and a neighborhood restaurant rather than a famous tourist branch. For BBQ, two people normally need 2–3 portions plus rice/noodles; `AYCE` rules often restrict time, meat selection, drinks, or leftovers.
* **Convenience:** neighborhoods are clustered around major subway/KTX stations. Use Naver/Kakao Map in Korea; Google Maps links below are useful for pre-trip planning but may have weaker Korean coverage.
* **Ordering:** show the Korean name, say `이인분` (two portions), and ask `포장돼요?` (takeout?) / `몇 시에 닫아요?` (what time do you close?).
* **Diet/allergy:** broth, marinades, kimchi, and banchan commonly contain seafood, pork, or sesame. Do not assume vegetarian means vegan.

## Flex-city decision

Choose **Daejeon** for the easier KTX stop, broad restaurant coverage, and Yuseong hot springs. Choose **Cheonan** for a smaller, less touristy stop and a food-focused visit (especially **Cheonan-style sundae**). From Seoul, both are straightforward day/overnight trips; do not split a short stay between both.

## Timing and booking checklist

* October 31 is Halloween weekend; expect crowds in Hongdae/Itaewon and reserve sought-after BBQ where possible.
* November 11 is Pepero Day; November weekends can be busy. Check whether a restaurant is closed on a regular monthly day, not just Sunday.
* Save the Korean name, phone number, address, and one backup per meal. Confirm last order, break time, and whether foreign cards are accepted.
* Recheck all facts against the linked business page, Naver/Kakao listing, and official tourism page in **October 2026**. A listing is not evidence that a restaurant will still exist.

## Research standard

`Official` means the restaurant/group or a government tourism site. `Trusted listing` means Naver/Kakao Maps or Visit Korea/Visit Seoul/Visit Busan. We do not present review-site hours as definitive. Where a price is not published by an official source, it is explicitly labeled an estimate and should not be treated as a quote. See [sources-and-notes.md](sources-and-notes.md).
