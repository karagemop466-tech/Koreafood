# Seoul Food Guide — Verified Entries Only

**Verification status:** every row below was checked against an official source (Michelin Guide, VisitKorea, or Visit Seoul) in August 2026. Entries whose opening hours could not be confirmed from an official source have been removed from this guide entirely, per the verification policy.

**No prices are listed.** Korean restaurants change menu prices frequently and almost no official source publishes them. Where Michelin publishes a price band it is shown as ₩ (inexpensive) or ₩₩ (moderate). Treat any price you see elsewhere online as unverified.

**Michelin note:** "Bib Gourmand" below means the restaurant appears in the *current* MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026 Bib Gourmand selection (51 restaurants). Three entries here hold that badge: **Geumdwaeji Sikdang**, **Hwangsaengga Kalguksu**, and **Pildong Myeonok**. "Selected" means the restaurant is in the MICHELIN Guide but does **not** hold a Bib Gourmand.
