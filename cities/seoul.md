# Seoul Food Guide — Verified Entries Only

**Verification status:** every row below was checked against an official source (Michelin Guide, VisitKorea, or Visit Seoul) in August 2026. Entries whose opening hours could not be confirmed from an official source have been removed from this guide entirely, per the verification policy.

**No prices are listed.** Korean restaurants change menu prices frequently and almost no official source publishes them. Where Michelin publishes a price band it is shown as ₩ (inexpensive) or ₩₩ (moderate). Treat any price you see elsewhere online as unverified.

**Michelin note:** "Bib Gourmand" below means the restaurant appears in the *current* MICHELIN Guide Seoul 2026 Bib Gourmand selection (51 restaurants). "Selected" means the restaurant is in the MICHELIN Guide but does **not** hold a Bib Gourmand. Several entries in earlier drafts of this guide claimed a Bib Gourmand they do not hold; those claims have been corrected.

### Also worth knowing

**Woo Lae Oak** (우래옥) is a **Bib Gourmand 2026** naengmyeon restaurant in Seoul. It is not listed above because no official opening hours could be sourced. Other Seoul Bib Gourmand 2026 restaurants you may want to research yourself include Hwangsaengga Kalguksu, Gaeseong Mandu Koong, Jaha Son Mandu, Gebangsikdang, Samcheongdong Sujebi, Okdongsik and 3rd Samgyetang. The full, current list of all 51 is on the [MICHELIN Guide Seoul Bib Gourmand page](https://guide.michelin.com/en/kr/seoul-capital-area/kr-seoul/restaurants/bib-gourmand).
