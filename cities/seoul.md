| Premium KBBQ | [Geumdwaeji Sikdang (금돼지식당)](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EA%B8%88%EB%8F%BC%EC%A7%80%EC%8B%9D%EB%8B%B9) | Bonsamgyeop (bone-in pork belly) **₩19,000**, Nunkkon moksal; estimate ₩35,000–55,000 per person | **Daily 11:30–23:00 (Last Order 22:20)**; Break 15:00–16:00 | [Official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/gold_pig1982/) · [Naver listing](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EA%B8%88%EB%8F%BC%EC%A7%80%EC%8B%9D%EB%8B%B9) · [Visit Korea](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/svc/contents/contentsView.do?vcontsId=191564) |

**Seoul note:** Tosokchon is convenient near Gyeongbokgung but queues; go before peak lunch. For premium pork, **Geumdwaeji Sikdang** is Michelin Bib Gourmand-recognized but requires waiting (CatchTable or registering early is recommended). For consistent thickness and table-side service, **Yukjeon Sikdang** is highly reliable.
