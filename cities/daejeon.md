# Daejeon Food Guide — Verified Entries Only

**Verification status:** every row below was checked against an official source (VisitKorea — the Korea Tourism Organization portal — or the restaurant's own site) in August 2026. Entries whose opening hours could not be confirmed from an official source have been removed from this guide entirely, per the verification policy.

**No prices are listed.** Earlier drafts carried "Est. Price Range" figures that came from review aggregators, not from the restaurants; they have been removed rather than shown half-verified.

**Michelin note:** the MICHELIN Guide's Korean selection covers **Seoul and Busan only**. There is no MICHELIN Guide coverage of Daejeon, so any Michelin claim about a Daejeon restaurant is false by definition. Earlier drafts also carried "Daejeon's certified Taste #1/#3" rankings that have no traceable official source; those have been removed.

## A note on what changed

Five of the seven Daejeon entries in the earlier draft survived verification, but **every one of them needed a correction**: a wrong district, an invented break time, a broken URL, a link to a festival page standing in for a restaurant page, or an unsourceable award. Two entries were removed outright. Treat any remaining unsourced superlative in this repo with the same suspicion.
