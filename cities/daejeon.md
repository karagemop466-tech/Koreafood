# Daejeon Ultimate Food Guide (Flex Option)

In **April 2026**, Daejeon Metropolitan City officially overhauled its local food brand system, designating **Bread**, **Kalguksu**, and **Dubu-duruchigi** as the official **"Taste of Daejeon" (Top 3 Tastes of Daejeon)**. This guide highlights the absolute best, most cost-efficient, and top-rated outlets for these regional specialties.
