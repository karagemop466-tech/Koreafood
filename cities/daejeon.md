# Daejeon (대전) — flex option

**Daejeon note:** Bread, Kalguksu, and Dubu-duruchigi are legendary here. For bread, **Sungsimdang** is a city institution that refuses to open branches outside Daejeon—expect lines but they move fast. For tofu, **Jinro-jip** is more comforting and classic, whereas **Gwangcheon Sikdang** is famously and intensely spicy. Swirl a kalguksu noodle sari into the leftover sauce to complete the meal!
