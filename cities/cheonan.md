## Byeongcheon-myeon (Sundae Street Area)

*Note: This area is famous across Korea for **Byeongcheon-style sundae** (made with vegetables and pork byproducts in a clean, savory broth). It is outside the downtown station core (about 30 minutes by taxi/bus), so plan transit accordingly.*
