# Busan Food Guide — Verified Entries Only

**Location index:** [jajangmyeon, KBBQ, galbijim, tonkatsu, spicy cold noodles, steak, local Korean food and cafes by neighbourhood](by-location.md) — restaurants and cafes in separate tables.

**Verification status:** every row below was checked against an official source (MICHELIN Guide, VisitKorea, or Visit Busan — the Busan Metropolitan City tourism portal) in August 2026. Entries whose opening hours could not be confirmed from an official source have been removed from this guide entirely, per the verification policy.

**No prices are listed.** Visit Busan does publish official menu prices for some restaurants, but prices change often and are not maintained consistently across the portal, so they are omitted here rather than shown half-verified.

**Michelin note:** "Bib Gourmand" below means the restaurant appears in the *current* MICHELIN Guide Seoul & Busan 2026 Bib Gourmand selection, which contains **20 Busan restaurants** (announced 26 February 2026). Several entries in earlier drafts of this guide claimed Michelin recognition they do not hold; those claims have been corrected. **One kept Busan entry now holds a 2026 Bib Gourmand: Cor Pasta Bar.** Nagahama Mangetsu is still dropped for unverifiable hours. Shinbalwon was dropped on this pass because two Visit Busan listings disagree on closing time. See the reference list at the end.
