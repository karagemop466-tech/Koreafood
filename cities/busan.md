# Busan Food Guide — Verified Entries Only

**Verification status:** every row below was checked against an official source (MICHELIN Guide, VisitKorea, or Visit Busan — the Busan Metropolitan City tourism portal) in August 2026. Entries whose opening hours could not be confirmed from an official source have been removed from this guide entirely, per the verification policy.

**No prices are listed.** Visit Busan does publish official menu prices for some restaurants, but prices change often and are not maintained consistently across the portal, so they are omitted here rather than shown half-verified.

**Michelin note:** "Bib Gourmand" below means the restaurant appears in the *current* MICHELIN Guide Seoul & Busan 2026 Bib Gourmand selection, which contains **20 Busan restaurants** (announced 26 February 2026). Several entries in earlier drafts of this guide claimed Michelin recognition they do not hold; those claims have been corrected. **None of the restaurants kept below hold a Bib Gourmand** — the one Busan entry that genuinely does (Nagahama Mangetsu) had to be dropped for unverifiable hours. See the reference list at the end.

## Removed from this guide, and why

| Entry | Reason for removal |
| :--- | :--- |
| Nagahama Mangetsu (나가하마만게츠) | **The Bib Gourmand claim is correct** — it is one of the 20 Busan Bib Gourmand restaurants in the MICHELIN Guide Seoul & Busan 2026. Removed anyway because Michelin never publishes opening hours and no official portal lists this restaurant, so the "Daily 11:00–20:30" in earlier drafts could not be verified. Worth going — **check hours on Naver Map the morning you go.** |
| Gaya Milmyeon (가야밀면) | Earlier drafts linked this to Visit Busan listing `uc_seq=102`, but **that listing is Halmae Gaya Milmyeon**, a different restaurant (kept above). No official page exists for a "Gaya Milmyeon" main branch, and the Gaegeum-dong original is widely reported to have closed for redevelopment. |
| Maga Mandu (마가만두) | No official source. The only link in earlier drafts was a generic Naver Map search URL, and neither VisitKorea nor Visit Busan lists it. Hours unverifiable. |
