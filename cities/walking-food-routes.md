# South Korea 15-Day Curated Neighborhood Food Walks

These day-by-day food routes are designed for **normal vacation days**, combining sightseeing, neighborhood walking, and high-intensity shopping with highly-rated, verified lunches, afternoon tea/cafe stops, and dinner destinations. 

*All listed restaurants and cafes are verified and correspond directly to our master list and `restaurants-bookmarks.csv` index.*

---

## 📅 Day 1: Seoul - Insadong, Bukchon & Traditional Jongno Culture Walk
*Explore Seoul's historic heart, walking through traditional hanok alleys, art galleries, and handicraft stores.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Imun Seolleongtang (이문설농탕)** *(Jongno)*
  * *Signature Order*: Ordinary Seolleongtang (₩12,000) or Special Seolleongtang (₩15,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Clean, comforting, 17-hour beef bone soup at Seoul's oldest registered restaurant (since 1907). Perfect energy-boosting fuel.
  * *Naver Link*: [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%9D%B4%EB%AC%B8%EC%84%A4%EB%86%8D%ED%83%95)
* **1:30 PM–3:30 PM - Activity (Shopping & Walking)**: Walk through the main **Insadong-gil** street, check out **Ssamziegil Shopping Center** (famous for local artisan crafts, pottery, and accessories), or walk up into the historic **Bukchon Hanok Village** alleys.
* **3:30 PM - Cafe Stop**: **Shin Old Tea House (신옛찻집)** *(Insadong)*
  * *Signature Order*: Hot Jujube Tea or Iced Omija (Five-flavor berry) Tea (₩8,000–12,000), paired with complimentary puffed rice.
  * *The Vibe*: A serene, 130-year-old traditional hanok courtyard cafe hidden down a quiet, leafy alleyway.
  * *Naver Link*: [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%8B%A0%EC%98%9B%EC%B0%BB%EC%A7%91)
* **4:30 PM–6:30 PM - Activity**: Walk around the majestic **Gyeongbokgung Palace** or browse the trendy, upscale designer boutique shops of **Samcheong-dong**.
* **6:30 PM - Dinner**: **Osegyehyang (오세계향)** or **Tosokchon Samgyetang (토속촌 삼계탕)** *(Jongno/Insadong)*
  * *Signature Order (Osegyehyang)*: Vegan Jajangmyeon (₩9,000) and Vegan Steak/BBQ.
  * *Signature Order (Tosokchon)*: Legendary thick medicinal Ginseng Chicken Soup (₩20,000).
  * *Proximity*: Both are highly central and within a 10–15 minute walk from Insadong.
  * *Naver Links*: [Osegyehyang](https://english.visitseoul.net/restaurants/Ohsaegyehyang/ENP001670) · [Tosokchon](https://www.tosokchon.com/)

## 📅 Day 2: Seoul - Myeongdong Shopping & Department Store Gourmet Loop
*Dive into Seoul's premier shopping district, exploring cosmetics streets, massive department stores, and legendary street food.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)** *(Myeongdong)*
  * *Signature Order*: Ordinary Gomtang (₩15,000) or Special Gomtang with brisket and tripe (₩18,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Historic beef soup landmark since **1939**, serving clean, deeply savory Hanwoo beef broth in hot brass bowls. Pre-payment required at counter.
  * *Naver Link*: [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%ED%95%98%EB%8F%99%EA%B4%80%20%EB%AA%85%EB%8F%99)
* **1:15 PM–5:30 PM - Activity (Cosmetics & Flagships)**: Spend the afternoon exploring **Myeongdong Shopping Street** (skincare flagships like Olive Young, fashion boutiques) and browse the basement gourmet and luxury floors of **Lotte & Shinsegae Department Store Main Branches**.
* **5:30 PM–6:30 PM - Appetizer**: Stroll through Myeongdong's vibrant **Street Food Alley** (active from late afternoon). Grab a quick *Hotteok* (sweet brown-sugar griddle pancake) or grilled lobster tail.
* **7:00 PM - Dinner**: **Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)** *(Myeongdong)*
  * *Signature Order*: Smoky chicken-pork *Kalguksu* (₩11,000) and a plate of juicy *Mandu* pork dumplings (₩12,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Fast-paced Michelin Bib Gourmand noodle house (since 1966) famous for its punchy garlic kimchi. Free noodle/rice refills on request.
  * *Naver Link*: [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EB%AA%85%EB%8F%99%EA%B5%90%EC%9E%90%20%EB%B3%B8%EC%A0%90)

## 📅 Day 4: Seoul - Euljiro Alleys & Dongdaemun Design Loop
*Uncover the retro hardware alleys of Euljiro, combined with cutting-edge architecture at Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP).*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Chanyang-jip (찬양집)** *(Jongno 3-ga/Ikseon-dong)*

  * *The Vibe*: Vintage plastic-stool noodle house since 1970 with a Michelin Bib Gourmand rating.
  * *Naver Link*: [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%B0%AC%EC%96%91%EC%A7%91%20%EC%A2%85%EB%A1%9C)

* **6:30 PM - Dinner**: **Yukjeon Sikdang Branch 1 (육전식당 1호점)** *(Sinseoldong)*
  * *Signature Order*: Premium thick-cut aged pork neck & belly (₩18,000–19,000/portion).

  * *Signature Order*: Specialty espresso (₩4,500–6,000) and freshly baked French/Korean pastries.

## 📅 Day 6: Seoul - Gwangjang Market & Dongdaemun Food Heritage Walk
*Immerse yourselves in Seoul's oldest market culinary secrets, from Michelin-rated raw beef to the famous One Chicken alley.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Buchon Yukhoe (부촌육회 본점)** *(inside Gwangjang Market)*
  * *Signature Order*: Fresh Hanwoo raw beef tartare plate (*Yukhoe* - ₩21,000) and *Yukhoe Bibimbap* (₩10,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Michelin Bib Gourmand recipient for 7+ consecutive years. Outstandingly fresh, fast, and cost-efficient.
  * *Naver Link*: [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EB%B6%80%EC%B4%8C%EC%96%B5%ED%9A%8C%20%EB%B3%B8%EC%A0%90)
* **1:15 PM–5:30 PM - Activity**: Walk around **Gwangjang Market** (grab some sweet, deep-fried twisted donuts), stroll along **Cheonggyecheon Stream**, and explore **Dongdaemun's historic fashion malls**.
* **6:30 PM - Dinner**: **Jin Ok-hwa Halmae Original Chicken (진옥화할매원조닭한마리)** *(Dongdaemun)*
  * *Signature Order*: Whole young chicken boiled in garlic-scallion broth (*Dakhanmari* - ₩28,000) table-side, paired with rice cakes and noodles.
  * *The Vibe*: Famous 1978 chicken alley. Hearty, delicious, and highly cost-efficient.
  * *Naver Link*: [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/p/search/%EC%A7%84%EC%98%A5%ED%99%94%ED%95%A0%EB%A8%B8%EB%8B%88%EC%9B%90%EC%A1%B0%EB%8B%AD%ED%95%9C%EB%A7%88%EB%A6%AC)
