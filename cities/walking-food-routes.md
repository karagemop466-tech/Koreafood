# South Korea 2026 Walking Food Routes & Customization Guide

These drafted food routes are designed for **normal vacation days**. They naturally balance sightseeing, neighborhood walking, and shopping with highly-rated, verified lunch, afternoon tea/cafe, and dinner stops. 

*All listed restaurants and cafes are verified and correspond directly to our master list and `restaurants-bookmarks.csv` index.*

## 🗺️ Part 1: Hand-Crafted Neighborhood Food Routes

### 🏛️ Seoul Route 1: Insadong, Bukchon & Traditional Jongno Culture Walk
*This route is a journey through historic Seoul, ideal for exploring traditional hanok alleys, art galleries, and craft shops.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Imun Seolleongtang (이문설농탕)**
  * *Signature Order*: Seolleongtang (₩12,000) or Special Seolleongtang (₩15,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Clean, comforting, 17-hour beef bone soup at Seoul's oldest registered restaurant. Perfect fuel for a long walking day.
* **1:30 PM–3:30 PM - Activity**: Walk through the main **Insadong-gil** street, check out **Ssamziegil Shopping Center** (cozy handicraft shops and courtyards), or walk up into the historic **Bukchon Hanok Village**.
* **3:30 PM - Cafe Stop**: **Shin Old Tea House (신옛찻집)**
  * *Signature Order*: Hot Jujube Tea or Iced Omija (Five-flavor berry) Tea (₩8,000–12,000), paired with complimentary puffed rice.
  * *The Vibe*: A tranquil, 130-year-old hanok courtyard cafe hidden in a peaceful back alley.
* **4:30 PM–6:30 PM - Activity**: Walk around the beautiful **Gyeongbokgung Palace** or explore the trendy boutique shops of **Samcheong-dong**.
* **6:30 PM - Dinner**: **Osegyehyang (오세계향)** or **Tosokchon Samgyetang (토속촌 삼계탕)**
  * *Signature Order (Osegyehyang)*: Vegan Jajangmyeon (₩9,000) and Vegan BBQ.
  * *Signature Order (Tosokchon)*: Legendary rich Ginseng Chicken Soup (₩20,000).
  * *Transit Note*: Both are highly central and within a 10–15 minute walk from Insadong.

### 🛍️ Seoul Route 2: Myeongdong Shopping & Central Street Food Route
*This route combines high-density shopping, cosmetics hunting, and department store browsing in Seoul's busiest central hub.*

* **12:30 PM - Lunch**: **Myeongdong Kyoja (명동교자 본점)** or **Hadongkwan Main Store (하동관 본점)**
  * *Signature Order (Myeongdong Kyoja)*: Hand-cut Kalguksu noodles (₩11,000) and a plate of juicy Mandu pork dumplings (₩12,000).
  * *Signature Order (Hadongkwan)*: Clean Hanwoo beef bone soup (*Gomtang* - ordinary ₩15,000, special ₩18,000) in traditional brass bowls.
* **1:30 PM–5:30 PM - Activity**: Get lost in the **Myeongdong Shopping Street** (skincare shops, apparel, flagship brands) and visit the magnificent **Shinsegae & Lotte Department Store Main Branches**.
* **5:30 PM–6:30 PM - Pre-dinner Walk**: Stroll through Myeongdong's vibrant **Street Food Alley** (active from late afternoon). Grab a quick street dessert like *Hotteok* (sweet pancake) or grilled cheese tteokbokki.
* **7:00 PM - Dinner**: **Menten (멘텐)**
  * *Signature Order*: Precise, Michelin-level *Shoyu Ramen* (₩11,500) or spicy *Tantanmen* (₩12,000).
  * *The Vibe*: A highly exclusive, precise one-chef Japanese ramen bar. The perfect reward after a busy shopping day.

### 🎨 Seoul Route 3: Youthful Hongdae, Yeonnam & Hapjeong Design Walk
*Perfect for checking out independent clothing boutiques, local designer labels, stationery cafes, and street busking.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Oreno Ramen Main Store (오레노라멘 본점)**
  * *Signature Order*: Tori Paitan Chicken Ramen (₩11,000). Ask for the **free** noodle or rice refill!
  * *The Vibe*: Michelin Bib Gourmand creamy ramen. Easy electronic kiosk ordering.
* **1:15 PM–4:00 PM - Activity**: Walk along the trendy tree-lined **Yeonnam-dong Gyeongui Line Forest Park** (known as "Yeontral Park") and explore the quirky accessory and stationery shops in the Yeonnam alleyways.
* **4:00 PM–6:30 PM - Activity**: Head to **Hongdae Shopping Street**. Browse indie fashion stores, check out street buskers, or visit local visual art flagships.
* **6:30 PM - Dinner**: **Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점0410)**
  * *Signature Order*: Cheap Jajangmyeon (₩6,500) and sharing chewy Tangsuyuk (₩15,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Chef Baek Jong-won's budget chain. Offers exceptional value, speed, and consistency.

### 🏮 Seoul Route 4: Euljiro Retro & Dongdaemun Shopping Route
*Explore the gritty, retro alleyways of Euljiro (hip-jiro), combined with high-intensity shopping at Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP).*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Chanyang-jip (찬양집)** or **Jongno 3-ga Grilled-Fish Alley**
  * *Signature Order (Chanyang-jip)*: Seafood Kalguksu noodles (₩9,000) with **free** noodle refills!
  * *Signature Order (Fish Alley)*: Charcoal grilled fish set with rice and sides (₩10,000–12,000).
* **1:30 PM–5:30 PM - Activity**: Walk around the iconic **Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP)**, shop at **Doota Mall**, or stroll along the scenic **Cheonggyecheon Stream**.
* **5:30 PM–6:30 PM - Afternoon Snack**: Stroll to nearby **Gwangjang Market** to snack on crispy *Bindaetteok* mung bean pancakes (₩5,000) and *Mayak Gimbap* (₩3,000).
* **7:00 PM - Dinner**: **Yukjeon Sikdang Branch 1 (육전식당 1호점)**
  * *Signature Order*: Premium thick-cut samgyeopsal & pork neck (₩18,000–19,000/portion).
  * *The Vibe*: Famous for professional staff grilling thick pork cuts with temperature guns. Highly rated and extremely juicy.

### 🥓 Seoul Route 5: Mapo/Gongdeok Food & KBBQ Alley Walk
*A local-approved, high-density route focusing on the iconic food and pork alleys of Mapo.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Sinseonggak (신성각)**
  * *Signature Order*: Handmade, old-school Jajangmyeon (₩8,000–12,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Revering local gem since 1981, known for completely hand-pulled noodles and authentic, non-sweet sauce.
* **1:30 PM–4:00 PM - Activity**: Walk around the beautiful riverside parks of **Mapo Han River Park** or explore local shopping alleys in Gongdeok.
* **4:00 PM - Cafe Stop**: **Fritz Coffee Company Dohwa (프릳츠 도화점)**
  * *Signature Order*: Drip coffee (₩5,000) and freshly baked retro-style pastries.
  * *The Vibe*: Located in a gorgeous, double-story converted vintage hanok, famous among local coffee purists.
* **6:30 PM - Dinner**: **Mapo Jeong Daepo (마포 정대포)** or **Budnamujip (버드나무집 마포)**
  * *Signature Order (Jeong Daepo)*: Pork skirt meat (*galmaegisal*) with cooked egg poured around the grill rim (₩18,000).
  * *The Vibe*: A high-energy, classic charcoal pork BBQ institution.

### 🗺️ Busan Neighborhood Food Routes

### ⚓ Busan Route 1: Busan Station Chinatown & Historic Market Walk
*This route explores Busan's gritty, authentic port-side heritage, vintage architecture, and giant traditional markets.*

* **11:30 AM - Lunch**: **Shinbalwon (신발원)** or **Maga Mandu (마가만두)**
  * *Signature Order (Shinbalwon)*: Crispy pork & chive Fried Dumplings (₩6,000–8,000).
  * *Signature Order (Maga Mandu)*: Silky, delicate pork & chive Steamed Dumplings (₩7,000–9,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Legendary, rivaling Chinatown dumpling joints located right next to each other.
* **12:30 PM - Cafe Stop**: **Brown Hands Baekje (브라운핸즈백제)**
  * *Signature Order*: Vienna Coffee or Café Latte (₩5,500–6,500).
  * *The Vibe*: Located inside a majestic, over 100-year-old brick hospital building built in 1922. Unmatched historic atmosphere.
* **1:30 PM–4:30 PM - Activity**: Walk/shop through the endless, lively alleys of **Gukje Market** and **Bupyeong Kkangtong Market**.
* **4:30 PM–6:30 PM - Activity**: Explore the sea decks of the sprawling **Jagalchi Seafood Market** and BIFF Square.
* **6:30 PM - Dinner**: **Hwaguk Banjeom (화국반점)** or **Choryang Milmyeon (초량밀면)**
  * *Signature Order (Hwaguk)*: Dry Black Bean Noodles (*Ganjajang* - ₩8,000) topped with a fried egg.
  * *Signature Order (Choryang)*: Refreshing, sweet, icy cold Mul-milmyeon cold wheat noodles (₩6,500).

### 🌊 Busan Route 2: Haeundae Coastal Breeze & Haeridan-gil Shopping
*A beautiful seaside route combining Haeundae beach walking, modern cafe-hopping, and coastal markets.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Haeundae Traditional Market** (vendors inside)
  * *Signature Order*: Grilled Sea Eels (*Gomjangeo*) or local milmyeon cold noodles (₩8,000–15,000).
  * *The Vibe*: A lively traditional market street steps from the beach, smelling of fresh seafood.
* **1:15 PM–4:00 PM - Activity**: Stroll along the wide **Haeundae Beach Boardwalk** or explore the trendy converted residential boutiques in **Haeridan-gil** (famous for stationery, photo booths, and indie clothing).
* **4:00 PM - Afternoon Ramen**: **Nagahama Mangetsu (나가하마만게츠)**
  * *Signature Order*: Authentic Fukuoka Tonkotsu Ramen (₩10,000) and crispy Gyoza.
  * *The Vibe*: Michelin Bib Gourmand ramen in a sleek, Japanese-style bar setting.
* **5:30 PM–7:30 PM - Sunset Activity**: Walk around the beautiful forest trails of **Dongbaek Island** to catch the stunning sunset over the ocean.
* **7:30 PM - Late Dinner**: **Tetsuya (테츠야 라멘)** or head to central Seomyeon for AYCE BBQ.

### 🏬 Busan Route 3: Seomyeon Central Shopping & Food Alley Route
*A high-energy metropolitan route centering around Seomyeon—Busan's downtown hub for underground shopping malls, local boutiques, and food alleys.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Songjeong Samdae Gukbap (송정3대국밥)** or **Gijang Sonkalguksu (기장손칼국수)**
  * *Signature Order (Songjeong)*: Pork Soup (*Dwaeji Gukbap* - ₩9,000) or Suyuk Baekban boiled pork set (₩11,000).
  * *Signature Order (Gijang)*: Chewy, hand-cut garlicky Kalguksu (₩7,000) and Gimbap.
  * *The Vibe*: Outstanding local landmark alleys (since 1946 and 1980s respectively).
* **1:15 PM–5:30 PM - Activity**: Shop through the massive **Seomyeon Underground Shopping Mall** (excellent for budget apparel, socks, and accessories), browse **NC Department Store**, and wander the independent designer alleys of **Jeonpo Cafe Street**.
* **6:30 PM - Dinner**: **Myeongnyun Jinsa Galbi Seomyeon (명륜진사갈비 서면점)**
  * *Signature Order*: All-You-Can-Eat marinated pork ribs (₩19,900).
  * *The Vibe*: Vibrant, bustling central Seomyeon BBQ house to end the day with unlimited grilled meats and local beer.

### ✈️ Busan Route 4: Sasang Riverside & Airport Gateway Walk
*An excellent route for arrival/departure days, or when visiting the western Nakdong River parks, centering around the Sasang Terminal shopping core.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Hapcheon Ilryu Gukbap (합천일류돼지국밥)**
  * *Signature Order*: Pork Soup (*Dwaeji Gukbap* - ₩8,500) topped with their signature heap of minced garlic.
  * *The Vibe*: Busan's most celebrated, rich, and intensely garlicky pork soup joint. Essential local eating.
* **1:30 PM–4:30 PM - Activity**: Walk around the lush trails of **Samrak Ecological Park** (right along the river, famous for cherry blossoms and reeds) or shop inside the massive **Sasang Apple Outlet Mall**.
* **5:00 PM - Pre-transit Dinner**: **Hong Kong Banjum 0410 (홍콩반점)** (Sasang Branch)
  * *Signature Order*: Jjamppong (₩7,500) and crispy fried dumplings.
  * *Transit Note*: Extremely convenient for heading straight to Gimhae International Airport via the light rail (just a 10-minute transit).

## 🗺️ Flex City Neighborhood Food Routes

### 🌰 Cheonan Route 1: Cheonan Station Market & Pastry Village Walk
*Explore Cheonan's oldest market alleys, followed by a pilgrimage to Korea's most famous fairytale bread village.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Daecheon Banjeom (대천반점)**
  * *Signature Order*: Jajangmyeon (₩5,000) and smoky *Ganjajang* (₩7,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Nostalgic, vintage market Chinese node where no menu item exceeds ₩7,000.
* **1:15 PM–3:30 PM - Activity**: Walk through **Cheonan Station Public Market** and pick up a box of fresh, hot walnut cookies (**Hakhwa Halmoni Hodu-gwaja** - 15 pieces for ₩5,000) right in front of the station.
* **4:00 PM - Activity & Pastry Stop**: **Toujours Dolgama Village (뚜쥬루 과자점 빵돌가마마을)**
  * *Signature Order*: Signature slow-fermented, sweet *Turtle Bread* (₩3,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Converted residential theme village resembling a fairytale Hobbit Shire. Perfect for walks, photo shoots, and pastry dining.
* **6:30 PM - Dinner**: **Menya Matsuri Ssangyong Main Store (멘야마쯔리 쌍용본점)**
  * *Signature Order*: Sizzling *Hwasan (Volcano) Ramen* (₩12,000).
  * *The Vibe*: 6-time Blue Ribbon Award winner. Serves self-made noodles in bubbling 300°C stoneware bowls.

### 🍞 Daejeon Route 1: Old Downtown "Taste of Daejeon" Overhaul Walk
*Experience Daejeon's certified Top 3 Tastes (Bread, Kalguksu, and Dubu-duruchigi) in a single, high-density old downtown walking loop.*

* **12:00 PM - Lunch**: **Jinro-jip (진로집)** or **Gwangcheon Sikdang (광천식당)**
  * *Signature Order (Jinro-jip)*: Silky *Dubu-duruchigi* (₩13,000–18,000) finished by tossing in boiled Kalguksu noodles (₩2,000).
  * *Signature Order (Gwangcheon)*: Famously fiery, firm tofu duruchigi (₩16,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Vintage, 50-year-old local institutions.
* **1:30 PM–4:00 PM - Activity**: Stroll through **Eunhaeng-dong's Underground Shopping Mall** and explore the trendy clothing boutiques and lifestyle stores of **Jungang-ro**.
* **4:00 PM - Bakery Stop**: **Sungsimdang Main Store (성심당 본점)** & **Cake Boutique (성심당 케익부띠끄)**
  * *Signature Order*: Crispy, deep-fried *tuigim-soboro* (₩1,700–2,000) at the main store, or elegant fruit tarts and roll cakes next door at the Cake Boutique.
  * *The Vibe*: Daejeon's proudest landmark. Expect fast-moving, high-energy queues.
* **6:30 PM - Dinner**: **Ossi Kalguksu (오씨칼국수)**
  * *Signature Order*: Hand-cut surf clam *Dongjuk Kalguksu* (₩8,500) and *Mul-chong* clam soup (₩13,000).
  * *The Vibe*: Extremely popular, steaming clam noodle bowls with fiery kimchi.
