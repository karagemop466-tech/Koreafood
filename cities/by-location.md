# Jajangmyeon, KBBQ, Galbijim & Cafes — by location

**This page is the organised index** for black-bean noodles (jajangmyeon / 짜장면), Korean barbecue, galbijim (braised short ribs), and cafes. Restaurants and cafes are in **separate tables**. Each table is grouped by neighbourhood.

**Focus this round:** Seoul — Myeong-dong, Gangnam, Hongdae, Dongdaemun — then Busan.

**Verification:** every row was read off an official page (VisitKorea, Visit Seoul, Visit Busan, MICHELIN, or the restaurant's own site) in August 2026. Address **and** hours both had to be on that page. No aggregator, no search snippet, no guessed Korean name. **Prices printed on official pages are not copied here.**

**Ten search passes, 19 August 2026 (64–73):** **+23 added** (Seoul +21 · Busan +1 · Cheonan +1).

**Ten further passes, 19 August 2026 (74–83):** **+21 added**, all Seoul — jajangmyeon, KBBQ, galbijim and cafes, still organised restaurants vs cafes by neighbourhood. Rejections are at the foot of this file and in the research log.
