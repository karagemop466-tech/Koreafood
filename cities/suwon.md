# Suwon Food Guide — Verified Entries Only

**Verification status:** every row below was checked against VisitKorea (Korea Tourism Organization) in August 2026. Hours and addresses come from those official pages. Entries without official hours are not listed.

**No prices are listed.**

**Michelin note:** the MICHELIN Guide's Korean selection covers **Seoul and Busan only**. There is no MICHELIN Guide coverage of Suwon.

**What Suwon is for:** **wanggalbi** (jumbo beef ribs) and **Suwon Chicken Street** (수원통닭거리) by Hwaseong Fortress / Paldalmun. Easy day trip from Seoul (Line 1 or KTX to Suwon).
