# Research pass — 4 August 2026

This pass confirms the city spelling as **Cheonan (천안)** and adds government-tourism evidence where available.

## Strongest newly verified facts

### Seoul

* Visit Seoul lists **Osegyehyang** at 14-7 Insadong 12-gil and specifically identifies vegetarian jajangmyeon and vegan BBQ. The page is old enough that hours and price must be rechecked.
* Visit Seoul lists **Donghung**, a long-running Chinese restaurant, with jajangmyeon, jjamppong, and tangsuyuk. It is a candidate for a future Geumcheon neighborhood section, but was not promoted to the main shortlist until current hours/menu are confirmed.
* Visit Seoul distinguishes Korean-style ramyeon from Japanese ramen; the guide should not treat those as the same meal. The main guide therefore labels ramyeon/ramen carefully.

### Busan

* Visit Busan lists **Gukje Milmyeon Main Branch** with mul milmyeon and bibim milmyeon at **₩9,000** in the listing, with address 23-6 Jungang-daero 1235beon-gil, Yeonje-gu.
* Visit Busan lists **Gaya Milmyeon** at 56-14 Gwangbok-ro, Jung-gu, and says it is open 365 days; current daily hours still need confirmation.
* Visit Busan lists **Hwaguk Banjeom** with daily hours 11:30–21:30, break 15:30–17:00, and closure on the first and third Monday. Its page recommends ganjajang and tangsuyuk.
* These official entries are stronger than generic “best of” blogs and are now in `cities/busan.md`.

### Daejeon

* The official Daejeon tourism site recommends the old downtown / Daejeon Station area for local kalguksu, and describes kalguksu, dubu-duruchigi, gukbap, and meat restaurants around Jungang-ro.
* This supports keeping Daejeon as the convenience-first flex city, but named restaurants still require exact Naver/Kakao verification before adding fixed hours/prices.

### Cheonan

* The official-source search was less useful for individual restaurants than Naver Map and Korean-language local references. The main guide keeps **Byeongcheon sundae street** as the city’s signature food route but correctly flags that it is outside the station core.
* A later pass should verify specific shops such as Cheonghwa-jip, Park Sun-ja Aunae Sundae, and Aunae Eomnamu Sundae using the city tourism database or their live map listings before assigning hours/prices.
